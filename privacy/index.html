Engineer Tycoon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Engineer Tycoon ("the game") is made by DoSa Games. This policy explains what data the game uses.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (your firm, engineers, currency and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive your save data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for in-game boosts. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when the game asks;
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and OS version;
  • ad interaction data (e.g. whether an ad was shown or tapped).

You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Buying No Ads in the shop removes ads entirely.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the game only which items you own so they can be restored.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your Game Center nickname, scores and achievements are shared with Apple to show leaderboards and achievements. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
